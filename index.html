02 · Annual maintenance

Skip service, skip your warranty.

Most manufacturers require annual servicing to keep warranties valid, and BC's Gas Safety Regulation puts real obligations on rental property owners to keep gas appliances maintained and documented.

We handle the service call and leave you with a record, so your appliance stays covered and your rental stays compliant.

Why annual service matters
Keeps manufacturer warranties valid
Most furnaces, boilers & fireplaces require documented annual service.
Required for rental properties
BC Gas Safety Regulation requires owners to maintain and document servicing.
Catches small issues early
Before they become a no-heat call in the middle of a January storm.
